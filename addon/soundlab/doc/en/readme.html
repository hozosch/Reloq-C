Eloquence C sound editor

The sound editor is a separate NVDA add-on. It requires the Eloquence C test build 1.5.0-dev191 or later. Restart NVDA after installing both add-ons. Open it from NVDA menu, Tools, Eloquence C sound laboratory, or press NVDA+Control+Shift+F10.

Load and edit a sound

Choose a language and sound, and adjust the voice, head size, sample rate, speed, pitch and acoustic clock if required. Choose “Laut laden” to generate the sound and move focus to the formant editing field. F2 automatically loads a sound that has not yet been loaded.

In the formant editing field, numbers 1 through 5 select a formant. Left and right arrows change its frequency in steps of 25 Hz, or 5 Hz with Shift. Up and down arrows change its level by 1 dB. NVDA announces the selected formant and its current value. Arrow keys work normally outside this field.

F2 plays the edited sound; F3 plays the original. Both keys work throughout the editor. You can choose to hear the isolated sound or the sound with its vowel context. For consonants, the engine generates a neighboring vowel; only the selected sound is edited. The context can make very short stop consonants easier to hear.

Formant frequencies are changed directly in the captured sound parameters. Eloquence uses a cascade of voiced resonances and has no separate level parameter for each one like eSpeakEdit. Level changes therefore use an additional filter around the selected resonance in the listening sample; they are not a parameter of an Eloquence sound file.

Import an eSpeak spectrum

For British English comparisons, select “Englisch (Großbritannien)” and the sound first. “eSpeak-Spektrum laden” opens an eSpeak or eSpeak NG spectral source file, which often has no file extension. Choose one active spectral frame from the list; the middle frame is selected by default.

The editor transfers the available F1–F5 frequencies from that frame to the midpoint of the selected Eloquence sound, retaining its original frequency trajectory and transitions as offsets. Missing peaks or frequencies outside the usable range are left unchanged and reported. eSpeak peak zero, levels, widths, fundamental pitch and the time sequence are not transferred automatically because the two synthesizers do not represent them in the same way. Use F2 and F3 to compare the result with the original Eloquence sound.

Use “Speichern” and “Projekt laden” to save several edited sounds and voice settings in a JSON project and reopen it. “Laut zurücksetzen” resets only the selected sound. “Textversuch” opens the previous laboratory for words and free-form sections.

Changes affect editor playback only. They do not automatically change the speech rules or the active NVDA voice. You can share the saved project for later comparison or integration.
