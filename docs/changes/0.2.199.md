# 0.2.199

- German identifier admission uses the existing punctuation classes at an
  extended-character boundary. A closing typographic quotation mark previously
  rejected a complete identifier and forced the whole utterance through the
  older fallback analyzer. That changed the reading of an earlier word in
  `„Vorbereitung“ wurde isoliert richtig gelesen. Das zuvor zerlegte „SAPI5“ veränderte seine Analyse`.
  Quoted identifiers now retain native whole-token analysis, including the
  source punctuation. No word-specific dictionary entry is added.
- The selected number policies are expressed in the shared German path:
  ordinary comma fractions name their digits, Euro/cent amounts retain their
  amount reading, and the r of vier-und belongs to the following onset.
  The authored cardinal data carries that onset directly. The previous outer
  adapter repair is removed; the native phonetic projection also supplies the
  selected liaison inside identifiers and filenames.
- Vocalic r after long ö retains its selected tract colour and uses the
  continuous nucleus source again. Its additional 8-dB source attenuation and
  the associated special attenuation ramps are removed.
- The German acoustic stages grow each target stream from actual use.
  A completed plan transfers the arrays to the renderer without copying them;
  an abandoned plan releases its private arrays. F0 retains its bounded
  phrase/inline-pitch reservation. Repeated-tick order, source timing and the
  existing fixed-buffer builder API remain intact. No speech-result cache is
  added.
- Community dictionaries are refreshed from the current master of
  `eigencrow/IBMTTSDictionaries`, commit
  `80eeab8c14e869e8edce03ec4d6908b71d35be33`. All seven files are verified
  against their Git blob hashes.

In the 4,000-word German case, requested live heap peak falls from
91,652,850 to 58,628,850 bytes: 33,024,000 bytes, about 36.0%, at identical
PCM output. The 196 language/rate/model PCM comparisons remain bit-identical;
46 selected German word pronunciations retain their SPR. The intentional
long-ö/r gain restoration retains sample counts in 21 rate/model pairs and
produces no clipped samples in that corpus.

For the reported quoted paragraph, median process CPU time to the first PCM
callback falls from 12.80 to 5.89 ms at 11 kHz, and from 14.29 to 5.91 ms at
48 kHz. Those are input-specific Linux host results. General CPU results
remain mixed, including a follow-up on one fixed logical CPU; they do not
establish an across-language CPU improvement.

The Windows DLL grows by 3,584 bytes to 55,226,880 bytes. The add-on grows
by 3,161 bytes to 19,856,092 bytes, including the fresh dictionary snapshot.
The APK stays at 17,708,438 bytes. This iteration reduces requested heap use;
compiled language code and constant data remain size-optimization targets.

Measurements, package hashes and validation limits are recorded in the
[validation report](../validation/0.2.199.json). Heap measurements count
requested live allocations on Linux/glibc, excluding allocator overhead,
stacks and RSS. CPU observations do not replace a Dell/ARM/Android device test.
PCM comparisons stay in RAM; no audio files are created.
