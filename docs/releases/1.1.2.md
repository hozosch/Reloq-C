# ReLoq C 1.1.2

ReLoq C 1.1.2 updates the NVDA add-on, Android app and Windows SAPI5 voices. The changes below are relative to 1.1.1.

## Changes since 1.1.1

- **Dashes before numbers:** a dash followed by a space and a number in running text, as in “Post - 0”, is treated as a pause instead of being read aloud. This addresses [issue #15](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/issues/15). Negative numbers and numeric ranges retain their previous handling.
- **ECI dictionary response:** reselecting a loaded dictionary and switching languages with loaded dictionaries no longer rebuilds their tables on every call. Dictionary edits and reloads still take effect immediately. This addresses [issue #17](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/issues/17).
- **Chinese and English:** transitions in both directions are shorter, while ordinary punctuation pauses remain. Embedded English remains neutral; Chinese words and numbers retain their normal stress and the selected inflection, including after longer English passages such as web addresses.
- **Long mixed Chinese text:** speech continues after repeated Chinese–English switches, including when NVDA speaks punctuation names within an English text, and Chinese speech resumes after number-containing passages and English sentence endings.
- **English words in Chinese:** words such as “clinton”, “microsoft” and “internet” reach the English pronunciation path regardless of capitalization, instead of being replaced with a Chinese transliteration.
- **German numbers:** pronunciation and transitions in number words such as “zweiundvierzig” and the eighties also apply when they form part of a thousand, for example 42000.
- **Community dictionaries:** the bundled optional dictionary snapshot has been updated to the current upstream version.

## Updating

The 1.1.2 packages update existing 1.1.1 installations on their respective platforms. If upgrading directly from the published 1.1 NVDA or Android package, see the [1.1.1 transition instructions](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/releases/tag/v1.1.1) for the one-time identity change.

The 1.1.2 downloads were replaced after publication to include the dictionary-response fix. If you installed the earlier 1.1.2 packages, download and install the updated package manually; an update check may not offer it because the version number is unchanged.
