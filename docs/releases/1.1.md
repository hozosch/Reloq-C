# ReLoq C 1.1

ReLoq C 1.1 updates the shared speech engine for NVDA, Android ARM64 and
Windows SAPI5. The German voice uses the native sentence and sound planner,
with pronunciation, number, question contour and dictionary improvements.
Long German utterances can begin speaking earlier. English time readings can
include seconds after the usual hours and minutes.

The accepted C tuning is the standard for higher synthesis rates; seven rates
from 8 to 48 kHz remain available. Higher rates provide additional consonant
bandwidth, with separate limits for Castilian
Spanish and Greek. German word-initial Z and ZW retain their male-voice level
adjustment. Inline voice controls are applied at word boundaries.

NVDA, Android and SAPI5 offer a choice of community dictionary sources; the
alternative IBM-TTS source currently covers US English and Castilian Spanish.
Personal pronunciations retain priority. On Android, the service and a
device-protected copy of enabled dictionaries are prepared for speech after
reboot before the first unlock.

The ECI interface includes the compatibility entry points and dictionary
handling needed by ECI hosts. ReLoq C is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later;
license notices accompany the packages and corresponding source is available
from this repository.

Install the package for your platform over the existing installation to
retain settings and dictionaries. NVDA 2026.1 or newer requires the x64
add-on; the Windows SAPI5 installer supplies x64 and x86 components. Android
uses the ARM64 APK. See the [1.0 notes](1.0.md) for platform requirements.

The reported rapid NVDA cancellation case has automated queue and recovery
coverage; confirmation in an actual NVDA session remains outstanding. A SAPI
comparison with mixed Mandarin and English plus word indices
has shown a difference from direct ECI and remains under investigation.
