# ReLoq C 1.1

ReLoq C 1.1 brings a substantial update to the shared speech engine for NVDA,
Android and Windows SAPI5. Compared with 1.0.1, it improves the sound at higher
sample rates, German pronunciation and responsiveness, voice controls and
compatibility with applications using ECI.

## Sound and performance

- The higher-rate sound has been retuned to address reports of harshness and
  crackling while keeping a clear voice and consonants.
  Higher rates provide additional consonant bandwidth, with a gentler upper
  range for Castilian Spanish and Greek. S and TS levels at the highest rates
  have been adjusted, and German F and V have more energy from 22 kHz upward.
- In German, initial Z and ZW are less prominent in the male voice; the female
  voice and other Z contexts retain their own levels.
- High-rate filters use less processing time. Actual responsiveness and sound
  can still vary with the device, voice, language and sample rate. The seven
  rates from 8 to 48 kHz remain available.
- SAPI delivers smaller audio chunks at the start and processes word-position
  events more efficiently in long utterances. The effect on startup time
  depends on the host application and device. A fresh SAPI installation also
  avoids repeated searches for settings from older installations.

## Pronunciation and reading

- German uses the shared native sentence and sound planner. Long German text,
  including long individual sentences, can start speaking sooner. Analysis of
  compounds, hyphens, slashes and pronunciation-dictionary entries has been
  improved, including compounds in quotation marks.
- German pronunciation has been refined in many contexts, including
  consonantal and vocalic R, final A, the glide in “Homepage”, nasal vowels,
  S and consonant combinations. Number-word connections, word stress and
  sentence melody have also been revised; questions receive a rising ending.
- American and British English now read the seconds in times and durations.
  For example, `13:28:25` keeps the usual hours and minutes and adds “and
  twenty five seconds”. Fractional seconds are retained as well.
- Additional fixes cover words that could previously be silent or incomplete,
  French `quil`, Spanish numbers and monetary amounts, and rising question
  endings in American English. Mandarin tone targets are more consistent across
  speaking rates and male and female voice models.

## Voice controls and dictionaries

- Volume changes now work in languages other than German. In NVDA and ECI,
  speed, pitch and volume changes within an utterance take effect at the
  relevant word or text boundary instead of being applied to the entire
  utterance. ECI voice tags can be used during speech without introducing the
  extra pauses previously reported for volume and speed tags. In NVDA, enable
  **Process ECI tags** in Speech settings if you want to use them in text.
- NVDA, Android and SAPI5 can now use **Alternative IBM TTS Dictionaries** as
  well as the existing community source. The alternative currently provides
  US English and Castilian Spanish dictionaries, but no German dictionary.
  It is downloaded in the background when first needed; personal entries or a
  custom SAPI dictionary folder retain priority.

## Android and ECI integration

- The Android settings interface is available in more languages. The speech
  service is prepared for Direct Boot: after a restart, it is designed to use
  protected copies of the selected settings and enabled dictionaries before
  the first unlock. A check on a physical device before first unlock is still
  needed.
- The ECI interface has been brought closer to the standard interface used by
  speech applications. Improvements include language discovery and switching,
  voice parameters and presets, text encodings, dictionaries, callbacks, error
  handling, phoneme output and pause/resume. Existing ReLoq text processing and
  pause features remain available. See the [ECI compatibility details](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/blob/main/docs/ECI-COMPATIBILITY.md)
  for supported behaviour and remaining differences.
- ReLoq C is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. The packages include the relevant
  licence notices and the corresponding source is available in this repository.

## Installation

The original 1.1 packages remain available from the [1.1 release](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/releases/tag/v1.1). For the later package identities and migration instructions, see the [1.1.1 notes](1.1.1.md).

## Known limitations and feedback

- A cause of the error reported when rapidly navigating long filenames has
  been fixed, but confirmation from the original reporter is still pending:
  [issue #10](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/issues/10).
- Feedback on the sound at higher rates is still welcome, particularly for S
  and the differences between rates: [issue #1](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/issues/1).
- Positions reported around a single quoted word can arrive at the end of the
  utterance. In SAPI, mixed Mandarin and English with word-position events can
  also differ from direct ECI output; this is still being investigated.

Please report other problems through [Issues](https://github.com/hozosch/Reloq-C/issues),
with an example, platform, language, voice, sample rate and dictionary setting.
