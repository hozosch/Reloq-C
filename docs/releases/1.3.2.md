# ReLoq C 1.3.2

ReLoq C 1.3.2 improves optional word segmentation and pronunciation, extends the shared settings to all three platforms, and improves update detection. These changes are relative to 1.3.1.

## Word segmentation and pronunciation

- The optional unfamiliar-English-word and multilingual CamelCase options now use the common speech engine on NVDA, SAPI5 and Android. Recognized compounds use their component pronunciations without an artificial word pause.
- Established English words and their inflected forms retain their native pronunciations even when their spelling resembles a compound. Existing pronunciation dictionaries continue to take priority, and native spelling of capitalized words is preserved.
- In NVDA, an object's name and role remain separate when segmentation is enabled, for example “YouTube window” and “YouTube document”.
- The built-in German dictionary pronounces “Cup” with a short a, including in compound words.

## Updates

The update check compares the version and the build time. Once this version is installed, a newer build published under the same version number can be offered as an update without repeatedly offering the installed build. Updating from 1.3.1 uses the ordinary version comparison.
